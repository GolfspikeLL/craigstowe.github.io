Privacy policy

Job Finder runs on your computer and uses your own Google account.

  • Drive: it creates a Job Finder folder and stores the resumes you approve. It only sees files it created.
  • Sheets: it keeps your job list in a spreadsheet in your account.
  • Gmail: it creates drafts for you to send, and it reads mail only to notice when a draft was sent.

This data stays in your Google account and on your computer. It is not sold or shared. To disconnect, remove Job Finder’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
